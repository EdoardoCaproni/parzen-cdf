\begin{abstract}
We train a sigmoidal multilayer perceptron (MLP) to approximate the cumulative distribution
function (CDF) of a known Gaussian mixture, using as supervision only the logistic Parzen-window
estimate evaluated at the drawn sample points, and recover the density as the derivative of the
learned CDF. This report grows one experiment at a time: each section states a hypothesis, fixes a
single experimental knob, and scores the result against the analytic truth. It is the LaTeX
counterpart of the Markdown track record in \texttt{docs/experiments.md}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Pipeline and constraints}

The pipeline is, end to end:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Choose a known mixture of Gaussians, with closed-form pdf $f$ and CDF $F$.
  \item Draw $n$ i.i.d.\ samples $X = \{x_1,\dots,x_n\}$.
  \item Fit a logistic-window Parzen estimator on the samples (unsupervised), with bandwidth $h$:
  \begin{equation}
    \Fhat(x) = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sigma\!\left(\frac{x-x_i}{h}\right),
    \qquad
    \fhat(x) = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{h}\,\sigma(z_i)\bigl(1-\sigma(z_i)\bigr),
    \label{eq:parzen}
  \end{equation}
  with $\sigma$ the logistic sigmoid and $z_i=(x-x_i)/h$. The logistic kernel is chosen because its
  integral is $\sigma$, giving a closed-form, smooth, strictly increasing CDF estimate; $\fhat$ is
  exactly $\mathrm{d}\Fhat/\mathrm{d}x$.
  \item Train a sigmoidal MLP $F_\theta$ to approximate the \emph{true} CDF, using the label
  $\Fhat(x_i)$ at each sample point $x_i$.
  \item Recover the density as $f_\theta = \mathrm{d}F_\theta/\mathrm{d}x$ (autograd), or via the
  copula (Sklar; multivariate, deferred).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Hard constraints.} (i) \emph{Train on the data points only}: inputs are exactly the $n$
samples $x_i$, labels are $\Fhat(x_i)$; we never sample $\Fhat$ at synthetic $x$, build collocation
grids, or augment the data. (ii) The learned CDF must be \emph{monotone non-decreasing}, enforced by
a loss penalty, a downstream rectification, or a monotone-by-construction architecture. (iii) The
network is sigmoidal and the targets are Gaussian mixtures.

\paragraph{Note on the inherited code.} The original training-set builder samples $\Fhat$ at
$\sim\!10^3$ uniform collocation points, which violates constraint~(i); it is retained only as an
out-of-constraint reference. All experiments below use the constraint-correct builder
$\{(x_i,\Fhat(x_i))\}_{i=1}^n$.

\section{Method and conventions}

\paragraph{Referee.} Because the data is synthetic, every estimate is scored against the analytic
truth on a dense evaluation grid. The grid and the truth are used for evaluation only; they are
never training data.

\paragraph{Metrics.} We report the Kolmogorov--Smirnov distance on the CDF,
$\KS = \max_x |F(x)-F_\theta(x)|$; the mean squared error of the pdf; the monotonicity violation
fraction (the share of adjacent grid steps on which the estimated CDF decreases); and the recovered
pdf mass $\int f_\theta\,\mathrm{d}x$, which should equal $1$. The Parzen estimate of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:parzen} is the target the MLP fits, hence the accuracy \emph{ceiling} for a given $h$.

\section{Experiments}

\subsection{E0 --- Naive baseline (data points only)}

\paragraph{Hypothesis.} A small sigmoidal MLP trained on $\{(x_i,\Fhat(x_i))\}$ recovers the Parzen
target reasonably; monotonicity may not yet be binding; monotone-by-construction (Sill) costs
accuracy.

\paragraph{Setup.} All four mixtures; $n=2000$; fixed Silverman bandwidth; one hidden layer of width
$16$ with sigmoid activations and a final sigmoid; Adam at learning rate $10^{-2}$ for $2000$ epochs;
seed $0$. Three monotonicity arms: \texttt{baseline} (unconstrained), \texttt{soft} (penalty
$\lambda=5$ on $\mathrm{relu}(-\mathrm{d}F_\theta/\mathrm{d}x)$ over the observed data range), and
\texttt{sill} (non-negative weights, monotone by construction). The density is the clamped autograd
derivative.

\paragraph{Observations.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \texttt{baseline} and \texttt{soft} are \emph{identical}: the unconstrained net already has
  zero monotonicity violations, so the penalty $\mathrm{mean}(\mathrm{relu}(-\mathrm{d}F/\mathrm{d}x))$
  is zero and contributes no gradient. Monotonicity is not binding at this scale.
  \item The net under-fits: the recovered pdf is a single smooth mode that misses the multimodal
  structure, although it fits its labels well ($\text{train MSE}\sim 3\times10^{-4}$). This points to
  the capacity and training-length axes.
  \item Two stacked sources of error: even a perfect fit to the Parzen target cannot reach the truth,
  because Silverman over-smooths the target. The bandwidth/target axis must improve alongside the fit.
  \item Sill underperforms on every distribution and loses the most mass ($0.82$--$0.88$): the
  non-negativity constraint costs expressiveness at width $16$.
  \item All neural arms under-integrate ($0.82$--$0.98$) versus Parzen ($\approx 1.0$): the learned
  CDF does not fully span $0\to1$ over the grid.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Verdict.} Baseline established in a clear under-fitting regime with an over-smoothed
target --- the headroom that Phase~1 (capacity, training, bandwidth) will probe.

\section{Planned experiments}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{E1 Capacity:} hidden width and depth.
  \item \textbf{E2 Optimization:} learning rate and epochs.
  \item \textbf{E3 Bandwidth / target quality:} Silverman $\to$ variance-matched $\to$
  cross-validation $\to$ adaptive (variable window).
  \item \textbf{E4 Sample count:} $n$.
  \item \textbf{E5 Monotonicity strategy:} soft-$\lambda$ vs Sill vs downstream rectification; pdf
  mass renormalisation.
  \item \textbf{E6} Interactions and best-of-axes pipeline; \textbf{E7} pdf-recovery refinements;
  later, multivariate / copula approaches.
\end{itemize}
