3 · Distill it into a neural network

An MLP regresses the Parzen CDF, trained only on the sample points (xi, F̂(xi)). The pdf is recovered as the derivative of the network. Watch what it learns, epoch by epoch.

Network & training

Sigmoid activations mirror the shape of a CDF; capacity must grow with the target's sharpness.

Width per layer. One hidden layer of 32 handles most smooth targets.
